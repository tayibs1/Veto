# 10-Hunch Graveyard & Statistical Deflation

To prevent multiple-testing data-mining (where testing dozens of strategies guarantees one will look good by pure luck), the Referee calculates the Effective Independent Trials ($K_{eff}$) from the correlation matrix of trial returns, and charges every trial against it.

### The Mathematics
- **$K_{eff}$:** $1 + (K - 1)(1 - \bar\rho)$, where $\bar\rho$ is the mean positive pairwise correlation of the $K \times M$ matrix of trial excess returns ($K$ trials, $M$ paths). Highly correlated trials count as fewer independent tests.
- **Trial-Adjusted p-value ($p_{adj}$):** Šidák correction, $1 - (1 - p_{raw})^{K_{eff}}$, where $p_{raw}$ is a two-sided exact sign test of wins vs losses against buy-and-hold.
- **Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR):** Bailey & López de Prado (2014). The probability that the observed Sharpe ratio beats the expected maximum Sharpe of $K_{eff}$ independent trials under the null, adjusted for skew and kurtosis.

### How to read this
- **H01, H02, H04 and H09 have small raw p-values because they lose.** The sign test is two-sided, so a strategy that reliably loses to buy-and-hold also looks "significant". SURVIVES also requires a positive median held-out excess (rule in `preregister/backtest.py`: median excess > 0 and $p_{adj}$ < 0.05), so these are rejected. DSR is reported alongside but is not part of the verdict rule.
- **H07 is underpowered by design.** Its holdout is cut to 12 paths. The referee returns UNDERPOWERED below 15 paths or 8 non-tied results, instead of judging a sample too small to detect a realistic effect.
- **H10 is a positive control, not a discovery.** Its holdout keeps only paths that end lower than they started, and fees are cut to 10p with 2 bps slippage. On those paths a cost-gated mean-reversion rule has a real edge. H10 shows that the gate can pass a genuine signal even after a $K_{eff}$ = 4.38 penalty ($p_{raw}$ 0.0003 → $p_{adj}$ 0.0012). It is not evidence that the rule works on ordinary markets. On unfiltered data the same rule family did not survive: see the 5-seed ablation (best $p_{adj}$ 0.0749) and the hidden challenge (12W/8L, p = 0.503).
